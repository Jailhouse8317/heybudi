12 week FYPJ

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** NYP students from all academic schools (SIT, SEG, SDM, SBM, SHSS, SAS).
- **Persona Focus:** Non-technical users seeking highly intuitive, practical everyday solutions.

## Core Objectives & Goals

- Equip students with the knowledge to deploy practical AI agents that automate complex tracking (e.g., extracting syllabus deadlines into dynamic assignment timelines) and optimize time management (e.g., generating personalized, bite-sized study schedules around core class timetables).
- Demonstrate how AI can simplify day-to-day coordination, from charting efficient, sheltered routes across campus hubs to effortlessly planning class bonding events and logistics.
- Bridge the gap between technical and non-technical students. By showcasing real-world, accessible applications, the project elevates the student body's AI capabilities, preparing them for an ever-evolving, tech-driven workforce.

## Notes

- **Democratized Access (Zero Cost):** Solutions must strictly utilize free or highly cost-effective tiers, ensuring financial constraints never limit a student’s ability to adopt them.
- **Frictionless UX:** Workflows require zero programming or complex setups—if a student can send a text, they can leverage **HeyBudi**.

## Final Deliverable

The primary deliverable will be an engaging, high-impact **promotional and instructional video campaign**. This video will introduce NYP students to the **HeyBudi** framework, visually demonstrating step-by-step how these AI solutions can be immediately incorporated into their personal and academic routines to save time and reduce stress.
