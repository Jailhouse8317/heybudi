## User Story 5: The Non-Technical Asset Sharpener (Upscayl Lifecycle)

**Persona:** Chloe, Year 1 student at the School of Business Management (SBM).

**The Story:** > As a business student compiling an official marketing case-study report,

> I want a way to take low-resolution, pixelated screenshots of company logos or market charts from the web and upscale them by 4x right on my laptop,
so that the visual evidence in my report looks razor-sharp and professional instead of blurry.
> 

**Acceptance Criteria (What your solution must do):**

- It must take a low-resolution, blurry web asset and scale it up 4x cleanly without introducing pixelated distortions.
- It must run entirely on Chloe’s local computer via a simple click-and-drop desktop interface, requiring zero cloud upload configurations.
- It must provide an intuitive side-by-side comparison slider so she can visually verify the sharpness upgrade before exporting the final image for her Word document.

**Target Persona:** Chloe (SBM)

**Objective:** Take blurry, low-resolution web screenshots of company logos or data charts and upscale them by 4x locally on a laptop—ensuring crisp, professional visuals for marketing case-study reports.

## Core Advantages of Local Upscaling

- **100% Offline & Private:** Because Upscayl runs entirely on your laptop’s local hardware, it requires **zero internet connection** after installation. Your school reports and data remain completely private.
- **Unlimited Use:** Unlike cloud-based AI tools, there are no subscription tiers, monthly limits, or credit countdowns. You can upscale thousands of images for free.

## Step-by-Step Execution

### Step 1: Initialize the Desktop Interface

1. Launch the **Upscayl** desktop application on your laptop.
2. The interface is highly streamlined, utilizing a simple left-to-right settings pane and a central viewing window.

### Step 2: Load Your Asset

1. Locate your low-resolution screenshot (e.g., a compressed brand logo or a blurry line chart exported from a web article).
2. Click the **"Select Image"** button on the left panel (or simply drag and drop your file directly into the application window).

### Step 3: Choose the Right Model for the Situation

Upscayl contains distinct AI models. Choosing the correct algorithm ensures text remains legible and shapes stay sharp without looking "fake." Adjust your settings based on the asset type:

- **Scenario A: For Brand Logos and Graphic Icons**
    - Set the model to **Digital Art** or **Ultrasharp**.
    - *Why:* These models focus on sharpening hard vector edges, smoothing out pixelated stair-stepping, and keeping solid corporate colors vibrant and clean without muddying the borders.
- **Scenario B: For Market Charts, Graphs, and Data Infographics**
    - Set the model to **General Photo (Remacri)** or **High Fidelity**.
    - *Why:* Charts often contain tiny labels, numbers, and thin grid lines. Remacri excels at preserving highly intricate details and enhancing local contrast, ensuring that the numbers on a financial chart stay completely legible rather than turning into blurry inkblots.
- **Scenario C: If You Are in a Hurry**
    - Set the model to **Upscayl Lite**.
    - *Why:* This processes much faster while still providing a massive quality leap over standard image stretching.

### Step 4: Run the 4x Upscale

1. By default, the scaling factor is set to **4x** (turning a tiny 400x400 pixel logo into a massive, crisp 1600x1600 asset).
2. Click the large **"Upscayl"** button at the bottom of the left pane.
3. Your laptop's graphics card will begin processing the image locally. A progress bar will track the rendering.

### Step 5: Verify via Side-by-Side Slider & Export

1. Once processing finishes, the central window transforms into an interactive split-screen.
2. Click and drag the vertical **comparison slider** left and right. Hover over small text or tight corners to compare the original pixelated version directly against the new AI-sharpened asset.
3. If the results look elite, click the **Download/Save** icon.
4. Open your Microsoft Word or dashboard report module, insert the newly saved high-resolution file, and scale it down to fit your page layout.
