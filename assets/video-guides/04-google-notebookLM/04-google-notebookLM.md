## User Story 4: The Socratic Study & Research Synthesizer (Google NotebookLM & Gemini Lifecycle)

**Persona:** Clarissa, Year 2 student at the School of Business Management (SBM) / School of Information Technology (SIT).

> **The Story:**
> *As a student overwhelmed by dozens of heavy lecture PDFs, industry reports, and complex module rubrics,*
> *I want to drop my course materials into a secure, grounded AI workspace to generate structured study guides, interactive flashcards, and Socratic audio overviews,*
> *so that I can deeply understand complex concepts and prepare for exams without getting hallucinated answers or wasting hours manually skimming documents.*

### Acceptance Criteria (What your solution must do)

* **Source-Grounded Accuracy:** It must analyze uploaded PDFs/notes exclusively within a private, zero-cost Google NotebookLM notebook without generating hallucinations or pulling outside unverified web data.
* **Socratic & Interactive Output:** It must automatically generate structured study briefs, key topic FAQs, and an interactive Audio Overview ("Deep Dive" podcast) based directly on the provided syllabus.
* **Seamless Export:** It must allow Clarissa to save or export synthesized notes directly into Google Docs or Google Calendar for immediate study scheduling.

### Workflow for Google NotebookLM & Gemini

**Target Persona:** Clarissa (Year 2)

**Objective:** Ground multiple lecture PDFs into Google NotebookLM to build an automated study guide, query specific topic logic via Gemini's Socratic engine, and export an Audio Overview for passive learning.

#### Step 1: Initialize the Grounded Notebook

* Open your browser and navigate to `notebooklm.google`.
* Log in with your account and click **New Notebook**.
* Upload your module documents (e.g., 3 weeks of lecture slides, project briefs, or PDF case studies).

> **Note:** Up to 50 sources per notebook are supported on the free plan.

#### Step 2: Generate Automated Study Assets

* Once sources are loaded, click on **Studio / Notebook Guide** on the right panel.
* Select **Study Guide** or **FAQ** to instantly extract key concepts, timeline dates, and core definitions directly from your uploaded files.

#### Step 3: Deep Dive Audio Synthesis

* Under the Studio panel, locate **Audio Overview**.
* Click **Generate** to spin up an AI-generated, two-host conversational podcast discussing the core themes of your notes.
* Listen on-the-go or download the audio file directly to your mobile device for revision during your commute.

#### Step 4: Socratic Querying & Export

* In the chat prompt bar, type a targeted query:
> *"Act as a Socratic tutor. Ask me 3 practice scenario questions based on Chapter 2 of my uploaded slides. Do not reveal the answers until I respond."*


* Once reviewed, click the **Save to Note** or **Export to Google Docs** icon to pin your study materials straight to your Google Drive ecosystem.
